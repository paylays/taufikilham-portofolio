# antislop report 002: minimal redesign

The owner asked for the minimal style of antfu.me and supplied new content: internship, teaching assistant and organization roles, thesis, project roles and user numbers, the myhotel project, and certifications. Direction and dials are in `DESIGN.md`.

Design Read: a personal portfolio for Indonesian tech recruiters, in a minimal, text-first language modeled on antfu.me at the owner's request, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

## What changed

- **Layout.** A plain header: name, three section links, language, theme, GitHub and LinkedIn. Below it, one 42rem reading column: intro, projects, experience, education, awards and certifications, stack and contact. On narrow screens the section links move to their own row.
- **Removed.** Sidebar, mobile drawer, tech grid background, mouse spotlight, custom cursor, scroll progress, marquee, scroll-reveal animations, gradient text and Lenis smooth scroll. The `lenis`, `gsap` and `motion` packages are uninstalled (`gsap` and `motion` were already unused). The 13 component, hook and CSS files that nothing renders anymore are deleted.
- **Data.** `portfolioData.ts` now keeps each project, role and credential once, with English and Indonesian side by side. That removes the drift between the two copies; for example, SITA-BPP was listed as PostgreSQL in English and MySQL in Indonesian, and it is MySQL.
- **Content added** from the owner's answers:
  - internship at Dinas Pariwisata, Pemuda dan Olahraga;
  - Teaching Assistant for Software Engineering and Cloud Computing;
  - HMSI ITK treasurer;
  - INSPACE sponsorship roles;
  - thesis title;
  - project roles (solo full stack, or frontend in a team for Kideco Matrix);
  - user numbers (40 to 60 students per period, 200+ residents);
  - INTERSI-ITK's four roles (it was listed as three);
  - the myhotel project, from its README;
  - the Dicoding JavaScript certificate.
- **Language and theme.** The site now opens in English, and `<html lang>` follows the language toggle. A small inline script applies the saved theme before first paint, so dark-mode visitors no longer see a white flash.
