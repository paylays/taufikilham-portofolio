# antislop audit 001

Date: 2026-10-17
Mode: after (audit first, fix only the numbers you approve)
Scope: everything that renders from `src/App.tsx`: Sidebar, mobile Navbar, About, Skills, Projects, Education, Contact, Footer, ProjectDetailModal, CustomCursor, MouseSpotlight, ScrollProgress, plus `index.html` and `src/index.css`.

How this was checked:

- Code inspection of every rendered component.
- Contrast ratios computed with the WCAG formula and cross-checked with `.claude/skills/antislop-human/contrast-check.py`.
- `npm run build` (passes; output inspected, then removed).
- No browser click-through or real-device test was run. Findings 13 and 14 come from reading the code and should be confirmed on a phone.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

---

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

**1. R-26, R-38: the contact form never sends anything.**
`src/components/sections/ContactSection.tsx:26-35`. Submit waits one second with `setTimeout`, then shows "Message sent successfully!" and clears the fields. Every message a recruiter writes is thrown away, under a subtitle that promises a reply "within 24 hours" (`translations.ts:69,158`).

**2. R-26: "Download CV" is broken in production.**
`src/data/portfolioData.ts:55` points at `/src/assets/Taufik-Ilham-CV.pdf`. That path only exists on the dev server. The production build contains no PDF, so the button returns a 404 on the live site (Sidebar and mobile layout).

**3. R-17, R-38: the "4++ projects" and "6++ certifications" metrics have no source.**
`src/components/sections/AboutSection.tsx:40-56`. The page lists 4 projects and 4 credentials (3 certificates and 1 hackathon award), and "++" is not a number.

**4. R-38: the Projects description promises a view switch that does not exist.**
`src/data/translations.ts:46,135`: "in slider or grid view". There is only one list layout and no toggle.

**5. R-02: em dashes in visitor-facing text.**
`index.html:7,10` (page title and meta description), `translations.ts:40,129` (Skills description), `portfolioData.ts:156,359` (Kideco short description), `SkillsSection.tsx:49-71` (all 23 proficiency tooltips).

**6. R-25: light theme secondary text fails contrast.**
`--text-dim` `#94a3b8` on white is 2.56:1 (needs 4.5:1), at `src/index.css:13`. It is used for the inactive sidebar nav links, the footer, contact labels, institution names, the "Relevant Coursework" label and the modal "TECH STACK:" label.

**7. R-25, R-34: dark theme small text fails contrast.**
`src/index.css:57-66`, measured on `#050507`:

- `--text-dim` `#64748b` is 4.28:1.
- Blue `#1d6ec7` is 3.98:1. It is used for education periods, "Live" links, the Skills and Education eyebrows and the modal "Project Overview" label.
- Garnet `#e11d48` is 4.34:1. It is used for the About, Projects and Contact eyebrows.
- Tech tags (blue on a blue tint) are 3.36:1.

All of these are small text, so they need 4.5:1.

**8. R-25: the contact form's success and error messages are hard to read in light theme.**
`ContactSection.tsx:157,162`: green `#22c55e` on its tint is 2.09:1 and red `#ef4444` on its tint is 3.29:1.

**9. R-25, R-34: the project modal title disappears in light theme.**
`ProjectDetailModal.tsx:87-88,125`. White title text sits on a gradient that ends in `--bg-card`, which is white in light theme. The bottom of the title lands on near-white, 1:1 in the worst spot.

**10. R-32: project details and scroll-to-top cannot be reached by keyboard.**
Project rows are `div onClick` (`ProjectsSection.tsx:53-56`), and they are the only way to open a project's details. The scroll-to-top bubble is also a `div onClick` (`ScrollProgress.tsx:45-46`). Neither one gets Tab focus, Enter or Space.

**11. R-32: the project modal does not manage focus.**
`ProjectDetailModal.tsx:38-60`. The modal has no `role="dialog"` or `aria-modal`. Focus is not moved into the modal or kept there, so Tab walks the page behind the overlay. Focus is also not returned to the project row on close. Escape does work.

**12. R-03: tap targets are under 44px.**
- `.btn-icon` is 40px, and 38px at 480px and below (`index.css:364-366,591`). It is used for theme, language, menu and social links.
- Copy buttons are 28px (`ContactSection.tsx:207-210`).
- The modal close button is 36px (`ProjectDetailModal.tsx:103-104`).
- The "Live" and "Code" text links are about 20px tall (`ProjectsSection.tsx:106-139`).

**13. R-03, R-32: skill proficiency only shows on mouse hover.**
`SkillsSection.tsx:137-184`. There is no tap or keyboard way to see it, and the section copy tells visitors to "hover".

**14. R-03: the soft-skills marquee is wider than the screen on phones.**
`AboutSection.tsx:189-190` sets `width: calc(100% + 5rem)` and `margin-left: -2.5rem`, but mobile content padding is only 1 to 1.25rem. The overflow is hidden by `overflow-x: hidden` on `html` and `body` (`index.css:90,100`) instead of being fixed.

**15. R-37: the site has no design direction.**
There is no `DESIGN.md`, no brand brief and no ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION dials. Most of the MEDIUM and LOW items below come from this, so any redesign has to start here.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**16. R-07: a dot-and-line "tech grid" covers the whole page.**
`App.tsx:18` and `index.css:130-137`. No identity reason is written down.

**17. R-01, R-13: glow appears all over the page.**
- A blue radial glow follows the cursor across the whole page (`MouseSpotlight.tsx:22`).
- Cards glow on hover (`index.css:266-274`).
- The progress bar and the scroll bubble glow too (`ScrollProgress.tsx:39,83`).

**18. R-01: gradient text is the default treatment.**
Every section title has one word in blue or garnet gradient text, and the mobile brand mark uses it too. The classes are in `index.css:148-164` and are used at `AboutSection.tsx:87`, `SkillsSection.tsx:98`, `ProjectsSection.tsx:36`, `EducationSection.tsx:128`, `ContactSection.tsx:52` and `Navbar.tsx:77`. The progress bar is a gradient as well.

**19. R-06: the fonts are the AI default roster.**
The site uses Space Grotesk for headings, Inter at weight 300 for body text and JetBrains Mono for labels (`index.css:32-34,99`), with no written reason. The fonts also load twice (`index.html:17` and `index.css:1`), and Syne loads without being used.

**20. R-06: uppercase, wide-tracked labels are used everywhere.**
An eyebrow like "GET TO KNOW ME", "TECHNICAL EXPERTISE" or "LET'S CONNECT" sits above every H2 (`AboutSection.tsx:67-77` and the same block in every section). The strings are typed in capitals in `translations.ts:24,37,43,54,61,113,126,132,143,150`. The modal adds more uppercase mono labels (`ProjectDetailModal.tsx:154-166,188-198,226-236`).

**21. R-19: motion is template animation, and reduced-motion settings are ignored.**
Every block runs the same slide-up-and-fade with a stagger (`index.css:443-483`). Lenis smooth scroll, the cursor, the spotlight and an endless 28s marquee (`AboutSection.tsx:202`) all run together. Nothing checks `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**22. R-10: backdrop blur is on three surfaces (the cap is 1 to 2).**
The mobile navbar (`Navbar.tsx:66-67`), the modal overlay (`ProjectDetailModal.tsx:48`) and the scroll bubble (`ScrollProgress.tsx:59`) all use it.

**23. R-14: the About cards are identical.**
The four highlight cards in a 2x2 grid share the same size, padding and hover (`AboutSection.tsx:114-145`). The three metric cards are also identical (`AboutSection.tsx:148-184`). Nothing shows which item matters most.

**24. R-04: Lucide icons are used by default, including the AI "Sparkles" glyph.**
- Each soft skill has a Lucide glyph, such as a crown for Leadership and a brain for Critical Thinking, and Sparkles is the fallback (`AboutSection.tsx:12-27`).
- Sparkles also sits on "Key Features & Capabilities" and a terminal icon on "Engineering & Architecture Highlights" (`ProjectDetailModal.tsx:255,304`).
- Every highlight line has a check circle (`EducationSection.tsx:96`).

**25. R-04: there are trophy emoji in the project copy.**
`portfolioData.ts:156,162,359,365`.

**26. R-01: a colored left stripe is used as decoration.**
The "Problem & Objective" box has a 3px garnet left border that marks no state (`ProjectDetailModal.tsx:217`).

**27. R-08: the arrow on project rows suggests the wrong thing.**
Each project row shows an up-right arrow (`ProjectsSection.tsx:79`). That arrow usually means "opens an external site", but the row opens an in-page modal.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

**28. R-30: the layout closely follows the most-copied developer portfolio (Brittany Chiang v4).**
It has the same pieces:

- a fixed left column with photo, name, role and tagline;
- uppercase nav links whose line grows on the active item (`index.css:197-242`);
- social icons at the bottom of that column;
- hover-highlighted project rows on the right (`index.css:390-427`).

This is the main reason the site reads as "another dev portfolio".

**29. R-05: every section is built the same way.**
Each one is eyebrow + H2 with one gradient word + muted description + content, with the same 6rem spacing (`index.css:431-434`) and the same entrance animation.

**30. R-29: blue and garnet take turns with no meaning, and there are extra colors on top.**
The two colors alternate by index or category (`SkillsSection.tsx:122`, `AboutSection.tsx:224`, `EducationSection.tsx:53-54`), so neither one works as the accent. Green, amber, emerald and Figma orange are added on top. `#004d98` and `#a50044` are FC Barcelona's blaugrana colors. If that is intentional, it can be the identity, but it needs one primary color and a clear job for the second.

**31. R-16: the copy relies on generic buzzwords, and the role title changes from place to place.**
- Words like "robust", "scalable", "high performance", "secure, fast, and scalable" and "Committed to high performance, system efficiency" (`translations.ts:18,31,107,120`, `portfolioData.ts:77,336`).
- The footer says "Built with Heart" next to a heart icon (`translations.ts:87,175`).
- The role is "Web Developer" in the sidebar, "Backend Web Developer" in the translations and "Software Engineer" in the meta description (`index.html:10`).

**32. C-4: switching to Indonesian only translates part of the page.**
- "Hire Me" is hard-coded (`Sidebar.tsx:139`, `Navbar.tsx:136`) even though `nav.hireMe` exists.
- The sidebar nav labels are hard-coded English (`Sidebar.tsx:11-17`).
- So are the modal headings, "Live", "Code", "Relevant Coursework:" and all the skill tooltips.
- `<html lang="id">` never changes (`index.html:2`).

**33. C-3, R-20: the strongest proof point is buried.**
2nd place at the KIDECO Innovation Challenge 2026 hackathon is a row inside "Education & Credentials" and an emoji line inside a project. Hackathon results and Google program names are also labeled "Relevant Coursework" (`EducationSection.tsx:8-14,82`).

**34. R-31: some effects have no stated purpose.**
- A 6px custom cursor replaces the system cursor everywhere, including text fields (`index.css:107-111`, `CustomCursor.tsx`), which makes it harder for low-vision visitors to track.
- A top progress bar and a floating percentage bubble show the same scroll position twice (`ScrollProgress.tsx:29-77`).
